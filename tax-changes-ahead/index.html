On the table: tax changes ahead

These changes have not started yet. They are in date order, and each says whether it is law, announced or being consulted on.

As of 8 November 2026, from the next date to the last one the calendar holds.

Law means it is in an Act. Announced means the Government has said it will happen but the Act is not final. Consulted means it is being discussed. This is general information, not advice.

  • 3 December 2026 Announced Scottish Budget sets the Scottish bands and rates for 2027/28
    Until then the app carries the 2026/27 Scottish bands over and says so. Scottish rates on savings, dividends and property income are not yet known. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2027 Announced Business Asset Disposal Relief: the lifetime limit falls to £500,000
    From £1m. The rate stays 18%. The app shows this as a note only and does not compute reliefs. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2027 Announced Child Benefit rises to £28.00 and £18.55 a week
    From £27.05 for the eldest child and £17.90 for each other child. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2027 Announced Company car tax on a zero-emission car rises to 5%
    From 4%, as a share of the car's list price. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2027 Consulted Founders' Relief: 10% on up to £10m of gains is being consulted on
    Proposed from 6 April 2027. Nothing is decided; the app does not compute it. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2027 Law Unused pension pots count towards the estate for Inheritance Tax
    For deaths on or after 6 April 2027 (Finance Act 2026). How this interacts with the nil-rate bands and with the tax beneficiaries pay on pension income needs a person to confirm. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2027 Announced Plan 1 student loan threshold rises to £28,005
    From £26,900, announced by the Government on 10 August 2026 for 6 April 2027 to 5 April 2028. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2027 Announced Plan 2 student loan threshold stays at £29,385
    It is reported to stay frozen until April 2030. The thresholds for Plans 4, 5 and Postgraduate have not yet been published for 2027/28. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2027 Announced Tax on property income rises to 24%, 44% and 49%
    In England, Wales and Northern Ireland. The rates for Scotland are not yet confirmed. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2027 Announced Tax on savings interest rises to 24%, 44% and 49%
    From 20%, 40% and 45%. The Personal Savings Allowance and the starting rate for savings stay as they are. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2027 Announced Trading allowance rises to £3,000
    It is £1,000 in 2026/27. Self-employed or side income of this much or less need not be declared. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2028 Consulted Dividend and distribution reform is being consulted on
    A consultation is planned during 2027, for changes that could start from 6 April 2028. Nothing is decided. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2028 Announced A tax on very high-value homes is announced for April 2028
    The detail has not yet been legislated, so the app shows no figure for it. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2028 Law Minimum age to take a pension rises to 57
    From 6 April 2028. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2029 Announced National Insurance relief on salary-sacrificed pension capped at £2,000 a year
    From 6 April 2029 only the first £2,000 a year of pension contributions made by salary sacrifice stays free of National Insurance; the rest is charged. Stated in two GOV.UK pages; not yet in an Act. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2031 Law Income Tax thresholds are frozen until 5 April 2031
    The Personal Allowance (£12,570), the basic-rate band (£37,700), the higher-rate limit (£125,140) and the start of the allowance taper (£100,000) do not move before then. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
  • 6 April 2031 Law Inheritance Tax nil-rate bands are frozen until 6 April 2031
    The nil-rate band is £325,000 and the residence nil-rate band £175,000, which tapers away above £2m. Source (opens GOV.UK or the publisher)
