Privacy

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and stores nothing on your device. It has no forms and no calculator.

The pages are plain files. They load no scripts, no fonts, no trackers and no advertising. Links to GOV.UK and other publishers open their own pages, which have their own policies.

The Overtaxed app works out your figures on your phone and never sends them anywhere. Links in the app to this website open in your browser, and only when you tap one.
